EXP and meso gain have compact fixed-grid overlays stacked at bottom left above
the native HUD, with independent checkboxes and two-second numeric updates.
EXP retains percentage/raw/both modes, session gain, recent pace,
current progress, timer and level-up ETA. Meso gain counts visible pickup-message
arrivals, including while EXP is unchanged; it does not read the wallet or estimate
profit. Known occlusion, reading pause and capture gaps prevent old messages from
being treated as new arrivals. Fast/obscured bursts may be missed. Actual game-log
and trade-window acceptance remain live checks, separate from fixtures.

The tracking page has an inset character/compact level badge, a rolling mm:ss or
h:mm:ss clock, aligned rate columns and inline coin units. Main Pause reading holds
tracking; the Prices shortcut remains independent. The Korean writer fades/slides
closed on Enter while its explicitly submitted draft finishes, copies to the
clipboard and shows an expiring game-overlay notice. It never pastes or sends keys;
Escape/edit still cancels pending copy authorization.

Prices reuse existing cards across geometry-only/irrelevant changes and repaint
only visible price/status/age changes. Recent items hydrate cached thumbnails and
preserve the last valid asking price across incomplete/covered/tooltip observations.
Selected cards place Overview/Sources higher, with compact source subtitles and
inset Overview facts. Named NPC shops and verified reported reward boxes appear
first, with images and database links. NPC prices never replace market prices.
All 44 current raw scroll records whose target was Other normalize conservatively
by matching ID family and canonical name; raw verified files are unchanged.
